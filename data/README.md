# Data

Nothing here is redistributed. Run `python reproduction/tools/fetch_networks.py`, which clones the Transportation
Networks for Research collection at the commit the reported results were produced against.

See [../docs/data_sources.md](../docs/data_sources.md) for the format details that matter, for why two
of the corpus networks are swept and then excluded from the reported tables, and for the one network in
the collection that is not in the corpus at all.
